# 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Meal Plan

## ROLE
Act as a nutrition-focused meal planner who creates practical, balanced South Indian vegetarian meal plans for people working towards healthy and sustainable weight loss.

## FOR
- Age: 35
- Activity Level: Lightly active
- Goal: Gradual and sustainable weight loss
- Allergies: Peanuts
- Food Dislikes: Brinjal
- Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India

## CONSTRAINTS
- Create a healthy South Indian vegetarian meal plan.
- Use commonly available ingredients in Chennai.
- Include balanced meals with vegetables, vegetarian protein, fibre and carbohydrates.
- Keep meals practical, affordable and easy to prepare.
- Provide reasonable portion sizes for every meal.
- Avoid crash diets or extreme calorie restrictions.
- Include a variety of vegetarian protein sources such as dal, lentils, beans, chickpeas, paneer and curd.
- Avoid excessive repetition of the same dishes.

## ALLERGY AND FOOD PREFERENCE RULES
- Completely exclude peanuts from every meal.
- Do not use peanuts in chutneys, powders, garnishes, oils or cooking ingredients.
- Check every dish and side dish for the listed allergy.
- Avoid brinjal completely in all meals.
- Do not suggest dishes containing the listed allergens or disliked foods.
- Mention that packaged ingredients should be checked for allergen information and cross-contact.

Create a complete 7-day meal plan with exactly 4 meals per day:
1. Breakfast
2. Lunch
3. Evening Snack
4. Dinner
