# Improvements Made After Run 1

After the first run, I improved the prompt to include better meal variety, balanced vegetarian protein sources and clearer portion sizes.

During the second run, I noticed that the AI included peanuts despite the allergy restriction. I strengthened the allergy instructions to ensure allergens are excluded from all dishes, chutneys and cooking ingredients.

The updated prompt was tested again with different activity levels, allergies and food preferences to check its reusability and consistency.s
