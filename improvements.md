# Improvements Made After Run 1

After the first run, I improved the prompt to include better meal variety, balanced vegetarian protein sources and clearer portion sizes.

During testing, I realized that the allergy instructions needed to be more specific. I added clear rules to exclude listed allergens from all dishes, chutneys and cooking ingredients.

The updated prompt was tested again with different activity levels, allergies and food preferences to check its reusability and consistency.
